Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Catalyst may collect, use, protect, and handle information when you use the platform.

Last updated: 8/15/2026

1. Introduction

Catalyst is a student productivity platform designed to help students organize their academic life, build better study habits, maintain consistency, and improve productivity.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you access or use Catalyst.

By using Catalyst, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

The information collected by Catalyst depends on the features you use and the stage of the platform.

Information may include:

  • Account information such as your name, username, or email address.
  • Authentication information required to secure your account.
  • Study and productivity information that you choose to enter into Catalyst.
  • Tasks, goals, notes, schedules, and progress information created through the platform.
  • Information related to focus sessions, productivity statistics, streaks, challenges, and achievements.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Information you submit through community or other interactive features.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure, and improve the platform.

3. Information You Provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when creating an account, setting up your profile, using productivity features, interacting with community features, or contacting Catalyst.

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless a Catalyst feature explicitly requires it and appropriate protections are provided.

4. Study and Productivity Data

Catalyst is designed around productivity and learning. Information such as tasks, goals, study sessions, notes, schedules, progress, and achievement data may be processed to provide the platform's functionality.

This information may also be used to generate productivity statistics, progress indicators, personalized experiences, and other features available within Catalyst.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Providing Catalyst's productivity and organizational features.
  • Saving and displaying user-created information.
  • Calculating productivity statistics, progress, streaks, and achievements.
  • Personalizing parts of the Catalyst experience.
  • Providing AI-assisted functionality where available.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of Catalyst.
  • Detecting misuse, abuse, fraud, or security threats.
  • Improving the platform and developing new features.
  • Communicating with users when necessary about their accounts or the service.

6. AI Features

Catalyst may include AI-powered assistance as part of its planned or available functionality.

When AI features are used, information provided to those features may be processed to generate the requested response or recommendation.

AI features should not be used as a place to submit highly sensitive personal information unless Catalyst explicitly states that such information is supported and appropriately protected.

7. Community and Public Information

Catalyst may provide community features such as discussions, study groups, shared resources, and other social functionality.

Information that you intentionally publish through public or community features may be visible to other users.

You should carefully consider what information you choose to share through these features.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Catalyst may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies where necessary to provide functionality and remember user preferences.

For example, Catalyst may store preferences such as your selected theme locally in your browser.

The specific cookies or technologies used by Catalyst may change as the platform develops.

9. Analytics and Technical Information

Catalyst may collect technical information about how the platform is accessed and used in order to maintain security, diagnose problems, understand performance, and improve the user experience.

Depending on the implementation, this may include information such as browser type, device information, operating system, approximate usage information, and technical logs.

10. Data Sharing

Catalyst does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers or third-party technologies when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve specific Catalyst features.

Catalyst may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, Catalyst, or others.

11. Third-Party Services

Catalyst may eventually use third-party services, APIs, hosting providers, analytics tools, AI services, authentication providers, or other technologies.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The specific third-party services used by Catalyst may change as the platform develops.

12. Data Security

Catalyst aims to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

Catalyst is designed primarily for students and learners. We recognize that some users may be minors.

Catalyst should not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would violate applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Catalyst in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

14. International Data Transfers

If Catalyst or its service providers process information in countries other than the country where you live, your information may be transferred and processed across borders.

Any such transfers will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Catalyst develops, introduces new features, changes its technology, or becomes subject to new legal or regulatory requirements.

When material changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

16. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or Catalyst's handling of information, you can contact the Catalyst team using the official contact information provided by the platform.
